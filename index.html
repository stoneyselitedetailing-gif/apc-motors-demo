About us

A family business that knows cars inside out

APC Motor Group is a family-run business with over 31 years of experience in the automotive industry. Our goal is simple: professional service and support whether you're buying any make or model, new or used, or selling your old car.

Owner-operator Angelo spent 13 years in vehicle sales, including as General Sales Manager for a major brand, picking up national sales and customer satisfaction awards along the way. He's also a qualified motor mechanic and a registered repairer with the Motor Vehicle Industry Board.
